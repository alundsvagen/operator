Questions about the events

How do I get a seat at an Operator Network event?

Request a seat on the event's Luma page, ideally at least two weeks before. The host approves every seat to keep the room small and well mixed. Approved guests get the address and time. There's no membership or application form.

Who are Insider Events for?

Founders, C-suite executives, operators and investors in Stockholm's SaaS, AI and fintech scene, plus senior product, engineering and go-to-market leads. Each event page says who that room is built for.

What does it cost to attend?

Most rooms are free. A few carry a small commitment fee, such as 100 kr for a breakfast round table. Any fee is shown on the event's Luma page.

How do I hear about new events first?

Follow the Operator Network calendar on Luma. New rooms are listed there as soon as they open. You can also email to register interest in a format, such as dinners or build days.

Can I speak or lead a discussion?

Yes. Most rooms are led by a few participants rather than a keynote speaker. Say so in your Luma request or email Alexandra. Speakers from our events are the guests we'll invite onto The Belonging Economy podcast.

Who runs Insider Events?

The Operator Network, founded by Alexandra Grisanti. Rooms are produced with Alexandra Studio, her B2B growth studio. Companies that want to host a room for their own buyers can do that through Alexandra Studio; those rooms carry the company's name as host.

Are the events only in Stockholm?

Almost all of them. The series is built around Stockholm; a few 2026 rooms ran in Gothenburg and Amsterdam.
