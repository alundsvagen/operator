About the network

What is the Operator Network?

Who runs it, who it's for, what happens in it, what it costs, and how it relates to Alexandra Studio. Plain answers, including what hasn't started yet.

By Alexandra Grisanti Published 22 October 2026 Updated 22 October 2026 9 min read

The short answer

The Operator Network is a Stockholm community for founders, C-suite executives and rising leaders in AI, fintech and SaaS. It meets in person, in small rooms: breakfasts, round tables, dinners and build days. It also gives people stages, through speaker invitations, panels and newsletter features. It is run by Alexandra Grisanti, who also founded the B2B growth studio Alexandra Studio and writes the Stockholm Growth & Fintech newsletter.

About 150 people are in the network so far. Joining is by application. Paid membership, planned at 850 kr a year, hasn't started yet. The Operator Network doesn't sell sponsorship: no company can buy a seat at its tables.

Who runs the Operator Network?

Alexandra Grisanti, a Stockholm-based founder. She also runs Alexandra Studio, a B2B growth studio that works only with fintech companies, and writes the Stockholm Growth & Fintech newsletter. Before that she worked in UX research and product design at the Stockholm fintech Zwapgrid, and as a designer at the marketing agency Heijbel Growth, where she started a CMO breakfast series. In 2026 she hosted the Nordic Fintech Highlights Stage at Nordic Fintech Summit in Helsinki and spoke on a panel at Stockholm Fintech Week.

The Operator Network is founder-led. It is not a separate company with its own staff, and it is not a second team behind Alexandra Studio. Her full background is on Who is Alexandra Grisanti?

Who is it for?

The network is for people who run things in Stockholm tech, and people who are about to. Applicants are reviewed against four criteria:

  • Experience: at least two years of professional experience.
  • Role: CEO, CTO, CFO or CMO, a manager position, or a technical expert position. You don't need to be C-suite; responsibility matters more than title.
  • Sector: AI, fintech or SaaS.
  • Location: living in Stockholm, because everything happens in person.

The point of the criteria is that everyone at the table works in the same world and carries comparable weight. That's what makes people willing to talk honestly.

What happens in the network

Rooms

Small, in person

Role-based breakfasts and round tables (a CFO round table, a CMO breakfast), dinners for 8–12 people, curated discussions under the Chatham House rule, and occasionally a full build day.

Stages

A platform to speak

Speaker invitations and panel seats at Nordic fintech conferences, discussion-lead roles at our own rooms, and photographs and write-ups afterwards.

Publication

A newsletter, then a podcast

Features in Stockholm Growth & Fintech, and a podcast featuring Nordic fintech companies coming next. Features are editorial and never sold.

Teams were mixed on purpose, so people built alongside other fintechs, banks and disciplines rather than their own colleagues. The day was produced by Alexandra Studio with the Operator Network. No hidden sponsors

The Operator Network doesn't sell seats, introductions or agenda time. If a company wants a room with a specific group of buyers, that work goes through Alexandra Studio, and the company is named as host on the invitation. Guests invited through the Operator Network should always know who is hosting the room they're walking into.

How to join

  1. Apply. The application takes a few minutes: who you are, what you run, where you're headed.
  2. Review. Every application is reviewed for experience, role, sector and fit. Not everyone gets a yes.
  3. Meet. If it's a fit, Alexandra books a meeting with you.
  4. Join the rooms. You start receiving invitations. When paid membership launches, you'll be told before anything is invoiced.

Questions people ask about the Operator Network

What is the Operator Network?

The Operator Network is a Stockholm community for founders, C-suite executives and rising leaders in AI, fintech and SaaS. It meets in person in small rooms, such as breakfasts, round tables, dinners and build days, and gives people stages through speaker invitations, panels and newsletter features. It is run by Alexandra Grisanti.

Who runs the Operator Network?

Alexandra Grisanti, a Stockholm-based founder who also runs Alexandra Studio, a B2B growth studio for fintech companies, and writes the Stockholm Growth & Fintech newsletter. The Operator Network is founder-led; it is not a separate firm with its own staff.

Who can join the Operator Network?

People with at least two years of professional experience who are CEOs, CTOs, CFOs or CMOs, in a manager position, or in a technical expert position; who work in AI, fintech or SaaS; and who live in Stockholm. You don't need to be C-suite. Every application is reviewed, and accepted applicants are invited to a meeting.

How much does the Operator Network cost?

Nothing yet. Paid membership has not started. The planned fee is 850 kr a year, with a three-month trial before the first invoice and a reminder email just before it. Some events carry a small commitment fee, for example 100 kr for a CFO round table breakfast.

How many members does the Operator Network have?

About 150 people are in the network so far. Paid membership has not started, so there are no paying members yet.

Can my company sponsor an Operator Network event?

No. The Operator Network does not sell sponsorship, seats or agenda time. Companies that want a room with a specific group of buyers work with Alexandra Studio, and those events run under the company's own name, so guests always know who is hosting.

How is the Operator Network related to Alexandra Studio?

Both are run by Alexandra Grisanti. Alexandra Studio is the paid service: it designs and produces rooms for fintech companies, hosted under the client's name. The Operator Network is the community and stage: it is where many invitations and speakers come from, and it is not sold. When an Alexandra Studio event uses Operator Network invitations, the invitation names the hosting company and says 'in partnership with the Operator Network'.

Is the Operator Network only in Stockholm?

Yes. Everything happens in person in Stockholm, so membership currently requires living in Stockholm.
