The Belonging Economy.

A podcast about life after AI, and why the people building it are meeting in real life again. Hosted by Alexandra Grisanti and recorded in Stockholm with the speakers from Operator Network events: the founders who demo, the operators who lead the round tables, the investors at the dinners.

Subscribe on Substack Pitch yourself as a guest

The idea

When AI makes everything else cheap, belonging is what's scarce.

AI can write, summarise, prospect and reply. More than half of web traffic is already automated. What it can't do is be known by a room of people who would vouch for you.

We call that the belonging economy: the part of business that runs on being present, trusted and remembered. It's why the Operator Network meets in person, and it's what this podcast is about.

What we'll talk about

Six conversations we keep having in the room.

01

Trust after AI

When a post, a pitch or a voice can be generated, what still counts as proof that someone is who they say they are?

02

The room as a moat

Why operators and investors are moving their best relationships offline, and what that does to deal flow and hiring.

03

Operator-led growth

Founders and executives who have built, sold or scaled, on how they now grow companies and back others.

04

What builders shipped

Founders from our demo nights and build days on what they built, what broke and who helped.

05

Belonging at work

Teams, communities and customers: what makes people feel they belong somewhere, and why it now shows up on a P&L.

06

Stockholm as a testbed

A small, dense tech city where everyone is two introductions apart. What the rest of the world can learn from it.

Questions about the podcast

What is The Belonging Economy?

The Operator Network's podcast and newsletter about the post-AI world and why people are meeting in real life again. It looks at trust, community and in-person rooms as AI makes content, outreach and even conversation cheap to produce.

Who hosts The Belonging Economy podcast?

Alexandra Grisanti, founder of the Operator Network, who runs its Insider Events in Stockholm and writes the Stockholm Growth & Fintech newsletter.

Who are the guests?

Speakers from Operator Network events on Luma: founders who demo what they built, operators who lead round tables, investors at the dinners, and jurors and partners from build days. See the upcoming events.

How can I be a guest on the podcast?

Speak at one of our rooms, or pitch yourself by email with your name, role and what you would talk about. You can also suggest someone else. Email a guest idea.

Where can I listen?

Subscribe to The Belonging Economy on Substack. Subscribers get the writing now and each episode as it is published.

Is the podcast part of the Operator Network?

Yes. It's the community's podcast: the conversations from our rooms in Stockholm, continued on the record.
