What you could build

Building the future of finance together, to solve real problems.

Every registered team gets the full inspiration packet with sources and brainstorming prompts. You are not required to use any of it. Arrive with your own idea and build that instead. This is just so nobody spends the first two hours staring at a blank file.

Problem 01 · Data fragmentation

The data arrives incomplete, inconsistent, or not at all

PSD2 mandated access. It never mandated standardisation. Around 4,491 European financial institutions each expose their own implementation, authentication flow and data shape. One bank returns the account holder name on roughly 81 percent of personal accounts, so about one in five comes back nameless. A Swedish company banking across three institutions still cannot get a reliable consolidated view of its own money.

Problem 02 · Authentication

Every product built on account data has a scheduled failure inside it

Consent has to be renewed on a clock, and on renewal day a meaningful share of customers simply do not come back. Reported drop-off has run above 50 percent in some businesses. What does renewal look like as a designed moment rather than an error state? And who re-authenticates when the account has three signatories and the person who set up the connection has left the company?

Problem 03 · Invoicing and fraud

Every control works exactly as designed, and the money is gone

Brå recorded 232,862 reported fraud offences in Sweden during 2025. A finance assistant pays a supplier she has paid thirty times before. Same logo, same layout, correct amount, correct reference. One field changed: the bank account number. Then there is the invoice that does not exist, and the receivable financed twice by two financiers looking at the same document.

Problem 04 · Sales and marketing

What if sales teams understood their customers through financial data?

Reps qualify prospects on a scraped headcount, a funding press release and a job title that was true eighteen months ago. Meanwhile the filings are published openly by the Swedish state. A qualification score built from actual financials. Trigger detection from filings. A churn signal from payment behaviour. A tool that tells a rep not to call.

Problem 05 · Financial decisions

820,000 Swedish limited companies, and the overwhelming majority have no finance function at all

The person deciding whether to hire, whether to take an order that requires buying stock, whether to pay a supplier now or in three weeks, is the same person doing the selling and the delivering. Their financial picture arrives weeks late, in a format designed to satisfy Skatteverket rather than to help anyone decide anything. In 2025, 10,731 Swedish companies went bankrupt, affecting 24,882 employees, and roughly one in four bankruptcies across the EU is attributed to unpaid invoices. Build decision support instead of reporting. Predict who pays late. Write the skattekonto as one plain sentence.

Frequently asked questions

What is Embedded Finance Build Day?

The first Nordic industry-wide finance hackathon. Mixed teams spend one day building working prototypes on real banking infrastructure at A House, Stadsgården 6, Slussen, Stockholm, on 25 August 2026, mentored on site by engineers from Danske Bank, Worldline, Froda, Open Payments and Zwapgrid.

Who organises it?

Alexandra Grisanti, founder of Operator Network, a closed community for senior operators in Stockholm's finance and fintech ecosystem, under Alexandra Studio. The problem set comes from research published in her Stockholm Growth & Fintech newsletter.

Which APIs will teams build on?

Open Payments for open banking account data and payment initiation, and Zwapgrid for ERP and financial system connectivity. Create accounts with both before the day and bring your questions. Rasmus Brenter and the Open Payments team, and the Zwapgrid engineers, are on site throughout.

Who is on the jury?

Dan Hyyrynen (Froda), Ola Hjort (Worldline), Christie H. Kristensen (Danske Bank), Louise Brandt (Open Payments), Mats Persson (Zinova), Rebecca Koskinen (Stack By Me) and Charles Arvidsson (AI Nordics). Eight teams present at 17:00 and the jury chooses a winner at 19:00.

Who can attend?

Invite only, for people already working in fintech, banking and adjacent fields. Developers, product people, designers, analysts and domain experts. You do not need a team or an idea, because teams are assigned as mixed groups across companies.

When does registration close?

21 August 2026, or earlier if the remaining seats go. Registration is handled on Luma.
