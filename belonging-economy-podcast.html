Questions about the podcast

What is The Belonging Economy podcast?

The Operator Network's podcast and newsletter about growth in a post-AI world, and why the people building companies are meeting in real life again. It hosts community leaders, thought leaders in growth, and the founders and operators behind SME business journeys, recorded in Stockholm.

Who are the guests?

Three kinds of people: community leaders who build rooms and networks; thought leaders in growth, from brand and demand to partnerships and pipeline; and founders and operators of small and mid-sized companies telling the real story of how they grew. Many guests are speakers from Operator Network events in Stockholm.

How can I be a guest?

Pitch yourself by email with your name, your company and the conversation you would want to have, or suggest someone else. Speaking at one of our events is the other way in. Email a guest idea.

Who reads The Belonging Economy?

470 subscribers on 24 November 2026, 97% of them on a work email across 353 organisations. 62% opened at least one issue in the last 30 days. Subscribers include people at banks and asset managers, payments companies, venture firms, SaaS and AI companies, and Swedish growth companies and SMEs.

Where can I listen and subscribe?

On Substack. Subscribers get every issue and each episode as it is published.

Who hosts the podcast?

Alexandra Grisanti, founder of the Operator Network, who runs its Insider Events in Stockholm. Her background.

Is the podcast part of the Operator Network?

Yes. It's the community's podcast: the conversations from our rooms in Stockholm, continued on the record.
