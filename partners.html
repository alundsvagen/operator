What partners do.

Partnership means building something together, not buying access. Three ways it works:

01

Build with us

Bring your APIs, sandboxes and engineers to a build day. At the Embedded Finance Build Day, Open Payments and Zwapgrid provided the banking APIs every team built on.

02

Bring your people

Lend your experts as mentors, jurors or discussion leads. Danske Bank, Worldline and Froda sent mentors to the Build Day, and their leaders sat on the jury.

03

Open a stage

Conference organisers and ecosystem groups give network members a platform: a panel, a delegation, a speaking slot.

Credit, yes. A paid seat, no.

Partners are named on the event page and thanked in the room, and they share in what gets built or discussed on the day. They don't get a seat at the table, a sponsored slot on the agenda or the guest list. That independence is the reason executives keep coming back.

Want to meet a specific group of buyers?

That's a different job, and it's done by Alexandra Studio, the B2B growth studio run by the Operator Network's founder, Alexandra Grisanti. Alexandra Studio designs a room around the accounts you name and runs it under your company's name, with a co-host your buyers already trust. Your company is named as host on the invitation, so guests always know who invited them.

Questions partners ask.

Can my company sponsor an Operator Network event?

No. The Operator Network does not sell sponsorship, seats or agenda time. Executives come to its rooms because no vendor pays to be there. Companies that want a room with a specific group of buyers work with Alexandra Studio instead, and those events run under the company's own name.

Did the Operator Network sell sponsorship before?

Yes. Until October 2026, this page offered paid, per-event sponsorship of closed executive events. That offer has been withdrawn. Executives come to Operator Network rooms because no vendor has paid to be there, so seats, introductions and agenda time are no longer for sale. If a search result still describes sponsorship packages, it reflects the old page. Companies that want to meet specific buyers work with Alexandra Studio, and those events run under the company's own name.

What does an Operator Network partner do?

Partners build rooms with the network. Technology partners bring APIs, sandboxes and engineers to build days, as Open Payments and Zwapgrid did at the Embedded Finance Build Day. Industry partners lend experts as mentors, jurors or discussion leads, as Danske Bank, Worldline and Froda did. Conference organisers and ecosystem groups offer stages where network members speak.

What do partners get?

Credit and a shared result: partners are named on the event page and thanked in the room, and they get the work built or discussed on the day. They do not get a paid seat, a sponsored slot on the agenda or the guest list.

How is the Operator Network related to Alexandra Studio?

Both are run by Alexandra Grisanti. The Operator Network is the community and stage and is never sold. Alexandra Studio is the paid B2B growth studio that builds rooms for fintech companies, hosted under the client's name.
