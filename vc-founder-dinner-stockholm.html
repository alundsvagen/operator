Questions about the VC Founder Dinner

When is the VC Founder Dinner?

Tuesday 26 January 2027, in Stockholm. Apply by Tuesday 12 January. The time and address are shared with approved guests.

Who is the VC Founder Dinner for?

Founders, investors and operators, especially people who have built, sold or scaled companies and now invest. The guest list is small enough for one conversation around the table.

Is it a pitch event?

It's a dinner, not a pitch night: one conversation around the table about what's working, what isn't and who should meet whom.

What is operator-led investment?

Investment led by people who have run companies: founders and executives who built, sold or scaled and now back others, as angels or in funds. Read more in Swedish on operator-led growth och investeringar i Sverige.

How do I get a seat?

Apply on Luma. Every seat is approved by the host, and capacity is limited. There's no membership and no membership fee.
