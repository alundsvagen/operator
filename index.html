Frequently asked questions

What is the Operator Network?

A Stockholm community for founders, C-suite executives and rising leaders in AI, fintech and SaaS, run by Alexandra Grisanti. It meets in person in small rooms and helps people build visibility through speaking opportunities, newsletter features and thought leadership.

Who is the Operator Network for?

Founders, executives and rising leaders who want to grow their professional visibility and industry influence, rather than relying on cold outreach or paid advertising alone. Applicants need at least two years of professional experience and must live in Stockholm.

Why does executive visibility matter?

Research from the Edelman–LinkedIn B2B Thought Leadership Impact Report shows that most decision-makers trust thought leadership content more than traditional marketing, and that a large share become more receptive to outreach from leaders who consistently publish high-quality insights.

How do I apply to join the Operator Network?

Applications are reviewed to keep the network curated. Visit the Apply page to submit your details. Membership is free so far; paid membership is planned at 850 kr a year.

How many people are in the Operator Network?

More than 100 members, and a wider network of more than 200 people in Stockholm who come to its rooms and events. Paid membership hasn't started, so nobody pays to be a member yet.

Can my company sponsor an event, or how is this related to Alexandra Studio?

The Operator Network doesn't sell sponsorship, seats or agenda time. It's run by Alexandra Grisanti, who also runs Alexandra Studio, the B2B growth studio that helps fintech companies build trusted relationships with the buyers they want to reach. Companies that want a room with specific buyers work with Alexandra Studio, and those events run under the company's own name. See Partners.
